## 1.3 Market observations

- **AI is now a mainstream QA requirement.** 5 of 10 postings require AI skills and 1 more lists them as preferred (6/10 mention AI). AI requirements appear from Middle level (#03, 2–4 yrs) up to Lead (#02, 8+ yrs).
- **Two distinct AI skill tracks.** All five AI-required postings ask for *using* GenAI tools (Copilot, Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT) to speed up QA work; only #02 and #03 also require *testing* AI features (chatbots, agents, RAG, hallucination detection).
- **Postings without AI** are mostly manual or test-design-centred roles (#06, #08, #09) plus an enterprise Banking/Insurance role (#07).
- **Salary transparency is low.** Only 3/10 postings disclose a figure; the disclosed range is **800 – 1,800 USD/month** for 2–4+ years of experience.
- **Common baseline skills:** API testing appears in 9/10 postings (required or preferred); English is explicitly required in 7/10.
- **Limitation:** all 10 postings come from one platform (ITviec) and one country (Vietnam), collected on a single day.
